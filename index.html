The Realm

dragon is asking to vote for you when you're away

If you agree, dragon's tool will cast your vote on bills and in elections that you haven't voted on yourself.

What stays yours

  • If you vote first, your vote stands. The tool skips anything you've already voted on and never changes a ballot you cast.
  • Every vote cast in your name is logged. Ask dragon to show you the log whenever you like.
  • Your password is never shared, and this doesn't reveal it.

Read this before you agree

The game has only one kind of permission for this, and it's broad: it lets the app do anything you can do in the game, not only vote. dragon's tool is built to send votes and nothing else, but you are trusting dragon with that.

Votes by every citizen are secret and final, so a vote cast for you can't be changed afterwards.

Changing your mind

In the game, go to You, then Build your own app, then Apps you've let in, and press Revoke. It stops working at once.

The game will ask you to press Allow. You can close that page if you'd rather not.
